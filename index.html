Privacy Policy

Effective October 16, 2026

Plain-English summary, not legal advice. Placeholders in [brackets] must be filled in before publishing.

What Nightline is

Nightline is a personal logbook for bets you place somewhere else. It never places bets, never takes or holds money, and never connects to or logs into any sportsbook account.

What we store

  • Without an account: everything stays in this browser on this device (bets, slip screenshots, deposits/withdrawals, settings, profile). Nothing is sent to our servers.
  • With an account: we store your email address and password sign-in (handled by our database provider, Supabase), your bets and results, the slip screenshots you attach, deposits/withdrawals (bankroll), your settings, and your profile: display name, profile picture and the Private setting, plus who you follow and who follows you.

Slip screenshots

Reading a slip happens on your device. The text-recognition program (Tesseract) is downloaded from a public code server (jsDelivr) and runs in your browser; your image is not sent anywhere to be read. If you save the bet, the screenshot is kept with it on your device, and when you're signed in it is also uploaded to your private storage folder so it syncs across devices. Only you can open it.

Who can see what

Your bets, slips, bankroll and settings are visible only to you. Other signed-in Nightline users can see your display name, your followers/following and, unless your profile is Private, your profile picture. Bet stats are not shared.

Outside services

  • Supabase stores account data and files.
  • ESPN public scoreboards are used to show games and odds and to settle bets automatically from final scores. The app requests these scores; your bets and personal details are not sent to ESPN. Odds come from ESPN's feed and are labelled with the book they come from (for example "Odds via DraftKings"). They are for reference only.
  • jsDelivr serves the app's code libraries (sign-in and slip reading).

Like any website, these services see your IP address and basic browser details when your device connects to them.

What we don't do

We don't sell your data, show ads, or share your data with sportsbooks or marketers. There are no third-party tracking or analytics scripts.

Your choices

  • Edit or delete any bet at any time. Export a copy from Settings → Backup.
  • Delete account (Settings → Account) permanently removes your bets, slip screenshots, deposits/withdrawals, settings, profile, follows and sign-in.
  • Without an account, Settings → Erase all data on this device removes everything stored in this browser.

Age

Nightline is for adults 18+ (or the legal gambling age where you live, if higher). We don't knowingly collect data from anyone younger.

Tips to keep it fun

  • Set a budget before you bet and stick to it. Only bet money you can afford to lose.
  • Never chase losses. A losing streak isn't "due" to turn around.
  • Treat betting as entertainment, not a way to make money or pay bills.
  • Take breaks, and don't bet when you're upset, stressed or drinking.
  • Use your sportsbook's deposit limits, time-outs and self-exclusion tools.
  • Check your Nightline totals honestly: if the number worries you, talk to someone.

Warning signs

Betting more than you planned, hiding bets, borrowing to bet, or feeling anxious when you're not betting are signs it may be time to get support. The helplines above can help, for you or someone you care about.
